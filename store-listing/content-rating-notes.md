# Content rating — notes for the IARC questionnaire (CalcWorker v1.0.0)

Expected outcome: **Everyone** (no age gate needed).

Questionnaire guidance (answer truthfully in Play Console):

- **Violence / scary content:** None. The app is a toolkit of calculators.
- **Sexual content / nudity:** None.
- **Profanity / crude humor:** None.
- **Drugs / alcohol / gambling:** None. (Finance calculators mention loans,
  interest, taxes — no gambling mechanics, no real-money wagering.)
- **User-generated content / user interaction:** None. No accounts, no chat,
  no comments, no sharing of user content. The AI helper on the site answers
  questions but publishes nothing.
- **Advertisements:** The loaded website displays Google AdSense ads. Ads are
  served by Google and are not part of the app's own content. (Keep the
  content-rating questionnaire's ads question answered as "contains ads".)
- **In-app purchases:** None.
- **Location features:** None (approximate location is used only by Google's
  ad/analytics scripts, not by any app feature).

No login, no social features, no user content → nothing that would push the
rating above Everyone.
